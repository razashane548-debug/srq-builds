Website privacy

A simple download website.

This notice applies to the SR Studio download pages, not a complete description of every app’s data use.

These pages

This site does not add advertising scripts, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the website, and GitHub Releases hosts APK downloads. Those services handle requests under their own policies.

External links

Downloads open a GitHub-hosted file. Contact links open WhatsApp, where the contact number may be visible. The studio website’s invite form uses a separate form provider.

This app summary is based on the public APK permissions and source review, not a network-traffic audit. It does not describe unreleased versions.

Stored on your device

The library, reading position, bookmarks, vocabulary, statistics, settings and voice notes are stored locally. Documents are chosen through Android’s file picker, which can include a cloud provider. OCR processing is local. This version has no account login, advertising SDK, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics or Firebase push messaging.

When information can leave your device

  • The update check contacts this site at most once a day. Hosting receives ordinary network information, but no book text is included in that check.
  • Online text-to-speech, including automatic voice selection when an online voice is used, may send text to your installed speech provider. Choose an offline voice to avoid online TTS processing.
  • System voice search may process speech through its provider.
  • Sharing or sending a report sends files you choose. Reports can contain book titles, file references, vocabulary and errors. Review them before sharing.
  • Backup exports go to the location or provider you choose. Android’s own cloud backup can also apply; app-lock preferences are excluded.

Permissions

The microphone is used for voice-note recording and system voice search. Optional accessibility-service access is enabled by you for volume-key reading controls. Notification, foreground media-playback, biometric and battery-optimization permissions support their related app functions. The public v0.3.4 manifest has no camera, contacts, location or broad storage permission.

“Stored locally” does not mean no information ever leaves the device. Your installed speech provider, Android backup settings and files you choose to share matter.

Permissions inside an app

Review the app’s permission explanations and Android’s permission controls. Do not assume this website notice covers an app’s voice services or other data handling.

Questions

WhatsApp messages only. Include your name, email ID and your query or complaint. Do not send passwords or sensitive files.
